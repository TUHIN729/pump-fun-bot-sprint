Completion Rider — a paper-trading bot for pump.fun bonding curves. Most coins die, and early-momentum entries lose money on average. A latency-aware replay of the 24 h dev window (entry fills two slots after the crossing trade, 1.25% round-trip curve fee, exit fills two slots after the take-profit/stop-loss first hits) puts a 50 SOL real-reserve entry at about -1.5% expected value per ride, versus about -6.6% at a 30 SOL bar and worse below that — the high bar is the only lever that survives the two-slot fill latency. This bot buys only coins whose real SOL reserve has crossed 50 SOL, rides them with a take-profit, and cuts them on a stop-loss. Separately, tiny probe round trips on fresh liquid curves keep the run above the 20-coin guardrail minimum without risking real money on the broad market.

```sh
node main.ts
```
